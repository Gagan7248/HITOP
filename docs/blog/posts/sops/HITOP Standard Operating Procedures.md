# HITOP Standard Operating Procedures

Welcome to the HITOP Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs) knowledge base. This section provides clear, repeatable instructions for routine tasks and operational workflows.

## Purpose

SOPs help teams perform tasks consistently, reduce errors, and maintain reliable work practices.

## How to Use an SOP

1. Select the SOP relevant to your task.
2. Review the purpose, scope, and prerequisites.
3. Follow the instructions in the listed order.
4. Verify the expected outcome.
5. Record completion or report any issue according to your team's process.

## Standard SOP Structure

Every HITOP SOP should include:

- **Title:** A clear name describing the task.
- **Purpose:** Why the procedure is needed.
- **Scope:** Who or what the procedure applies to.
- **Prerequisites:** Required access, tools, or approvals.
- **Procedure:** Numbered, actionable steps.
- **Verification:** How to confirm successful completion.
- **Troubleshooting:** Common issues and their resolution.
- **Revision history:** Date and summary of changes.

## Important Notes

- Follow approved procedures and applicable security requirements.
- Do not skip verification steps.
- If an instruction is outdated or unclear, report it to the document owner.
- Only authorized owners should approve changes to controlled SOPs.
