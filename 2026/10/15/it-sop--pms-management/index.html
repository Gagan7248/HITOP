    IT SOP – PMS Management

    Purpose

    To establish a formal procedure for the daily management, maintenance, and operational integrity of the Property Management System (PMS), ensuring seamless Front Office operations, system availability, and accurate guest data management.

    Scope

    This SOP applies to all members of the IT Department responsible for administering, monitoring, securing, maintaining, and troubleshooting the Property Management System (PMS) and its integrated systems.

    Responsibility

    • Execution: IT Manager and IT Executive.

    • Monitoring: IT Manager.

    Procedure

    1. Perform a daily system health check of the PMS server, connected terminals, and associated services.

    2. Verify the successful completion of the nightly audit and scheduled data backups.

    3. Review system logs for errors, warnings, unusual activities, and unauthorized access attempts.

    4. Monitor PMS integrations, including the Point of Sale (POS), key card system, and Private Branch Exchange (PBX), to confirm that all interfaces are online and communicating correctly.

    5. Create, modify, and deactivate PMS user accounts according to approved requests and employee roles.

    6. Verify that appropriate access permissions are assigned to authorized personnel based on their job responsibilities.

    7. Coordinate with the Front Office and Reservations teams to investigate and resolve PMS-related issues promptly.

    8. Record all PMS incidents, troubleshooting activities, and corrective actions in the IT ticketing system.

    9. Apply approved software patches and updates during off-peak hours, following vendor recommendations and change management procedures.

    10. Verify system functionality after maintenance or updates and confirm that critical PMS services are operating normally.

    11. Escalate major incidents, recurring errors, and potential data integrity issues to the IT Manager and relevant stakeholders.

    12. Maintain daily health check reports, access records, maintenance logs, and backup verification records.

    Standards & Guidelines

    • PMS access must be granted according to the principle of least privilege and business requirements.

    • Routine maintenance should be scheduled during low-occupancy or off-peak periods to minimize operational disruption.

    • The target PMS system availability should be 99.9% or higher, subject to approved service requirements and vendor capabilities.

    • All PMS-related incidents, service requests, and changes must be documented in the IT ticketing system.

    • Software updates and configuration changes must be authorized and performed according to approved change management procedures.

    • User accounts must be reviewed periodically, and access must be revoked promptly when no longer required.

    • Data backups must be monitored regularly, and restoration procedures should be tested according to the hotel's backup policy.

    • Any suspected security incident or guest data exposure must be reported immediately through the hotel's incident escalation process.

    Records & Documentation

    The following records must be maintained:

    • Daily PMS system health check reports.

    • IT ticketing system logs for PMS-related incidents and service requests.

    • PMS user access records and change logs.

    • Nightly audit verification reports.

    • Data backup completion and verification reports.

    • Software update and maintenance records.

    • PMS incident investigation and resolution reports.

    • System availability and downtime records.

    Safety & Precautions

    • Never share, disclose, or reuse PMS login credentials in an unauthorized manner.

    • Use strong passwords and approved authentication controls wherever supported.

    • Handle guest information with strict confidentiality and in accordance with applicable data protection requirements and hotel policies.

    • Do not make unauthorized changes to PMS configurations, user permissions, or integrated systems.

    • Verify backup completion before performing maintenance that could affect system data or availability.

    • Maintenance requiring system downtime must be communicated in advance and approved by the General Manager and relevant Department Heads.

    • Follow the hotel's incident response and escalation procedures in the event of system failure, suspected unauthorized access, or data loss.

    • Ensure that critical PMS functions are verified after maintenance before returning the system to normal operation.
