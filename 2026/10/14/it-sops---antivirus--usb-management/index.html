    IT SOPs – Antivirus & USB Management

    1. Purpose

    To protect the resort’s network and computing infrastructure from malware, viruses, and unauthorized data transfers through effective antivirus protection and USB device management.

    2. Scope

    This SOP applies to all IT Department personnel and covers all resort-owned computing devices, including servers, desktops, laptops, and Point of Sale (POS) terminals.

    3. Responsibility

    • Execution: IT Executive

    • Monitoring and Compliance: IT Manager

    4. Procedure

    4.1 Antivirus Management

    1. Ensure that approved antivirus software is installed and active on all resort-owned computing devices.

    2. Update antivirus definitions automatically wherever possible, or perform manual updates when required.

    3. Perform weekly full system scans on all mission-critical servers and desktops.

    4. Monitor the centralized antivirus management console for security alerts, detected threats, quarantined files, and scan status.

    5. Investigate and remediate detected threats according to the resort’s IT security procedures.

    6. Immediately isolate any device suspected of being infected to prevent the spread of malware across the network.

    4.2 USB Device Management

    1. Disable USB autorun functionality on all applicable resort-owned computing devices.

    2. Permit only IT-approved, scanned, and whitelisted USB storage devices.

    3. Scan every USB storage device using approved antivirus software before accessing or transferring files.

    4. Prohibit the use of personal or unauthorized USB storage devices on resort computers.

    5. Maintain an official whitelist of approved USB devices, including device identification details and authorization status.

    6. Remove or revoke authorization for USB devices that are lost, compromised, or no longer required.

    5. Standards & Guidelines

    • Antivirus definitions must be updated at least once every 24 hours.

    • Antivirus protection must remain enabled and operational on all supported devices.

    • Weekly full system scans must be completed and documented for mission-critical servers and desktops.

    • Devices with detected or suspected malware must be isolated from the network immediately, following established incident response procedures.

    • Personal and unauthorized USB storage devices must not be connected to resort-owned computers.

    • Only authorized and whitelisted USB devices may be used for business purposes.

    • The IT Department must maintain an up-to-date USB device whitelist.

    • Any antivirus failure, suspicious activity, or security incident must be reported promptly to the IT Manager.

    6. Records & Documentation

    The IT Department must maintain the following records:

    • Centralized antivirus console reports, including detected threats, quarantined files, and scan history.

    • Antivirus definition update logs and records of manual updates.

    • Full system scan logs and remediation records.

    • Approved USB device whitelist and authorization records.

    • Security incident reports and corrective actions taken.

    7. Safety & Precautions

    1. In the event of a suspected malware outbreak, immediately isolate affected devices from the network to prevent lateral spread.

    2. Do not delete quarantined files or disable antivirus protection without proper authorization.

    3. Do not connect unknown, personal, or unapproved USB storage devices to resort-owned computers.

    4. Report serious security incidents immediately to the IT Manager and General Manager.

    5. Preserve relevant security logs and evidence for investigation, where applicable.

    8. Compliance

    All IT personnel must follow this SOP. Any exception to antivirus or USB security controls requires prior authorization from the IT Manager and must be documented.
