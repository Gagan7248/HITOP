    POS OPERATIONS & SUPPORT

    Department: Information Technology (IT)
    SOP Category: Hotel Application Systems
    SOP Number: SOP-002 Document Type: Standard Operating Procedure
    Applicable To: Hotels, Resorts, Restaurants, Bars, Banquet Operations and Other Food & Beverage Outlets
    Process Owner: IT Department
    Review Frequency: Annually or whenever a major operational or system change occurs
    Version: 1.0
    Effective Date: As approved by hotel management

    1. PURPOSE

    The purpose of this Standard Operating Procedure is to establish a consistent process for the operation, monitoring, maintenance, troubleshooting, and support of the hotel's Point of Sale (POS) system.

    The POS system is an essential operational application used by Food & Beverage outlets to record orders, process payments, manage billing, and transfer charges to guest accounts through integration with the Property Management System (PMS).

    This SOP ensures:

    • Reliable availability of POS services.

    • Accurate order entry and billing operations.

    • Proper integration between POS and PMS.

    • Controlled user access and permissions.

    • Timely resolution of technical issues.

    • Secure handling of transaction and guest information.

    • Proper coordination between IT, Food & Beverage, Finance, and system vendors.

    • Consistent documentation of system activities and incidents.

    2. SCOPE

    This SOP applies to all POS systems and related equipment used within the hotel.

    It covers:

    • Restaurant POS terminals.

    • Bar and lounge POS terminals.

    • Room service POS terminals.

    • Banquet and event POS terminals.

    • Poolside and outdoor POS terminals.

    • Kitchen order display systems (KDS).

    • POS printers and kitchen printers.

    • Handheld POS devices and tablets.

    • POS software and databases.

    • POS application servers or cloud services.

    • POS-to-PMS integration.

    • POS network connectivity.

    • Payment terminal connectivity, where applicable.

    • POS user accounts and access permissions.

    • POS backups, updates, and vendor support.

    This SOP applies whether the POS system is cloud-based, locally hosted, centrally hosted, or integrated with other hotel applications.

    3. RESPONSIBILITIES

    3.1 IT Manager / IT Head

    • Overall ownership of POS system technical operations.

    • Ensure POS availability and support arrangements.

    • Approve technical changes and access requests according to hotel policy.

    • Coordinate with vendors for major technical issues.

    • Monitor recurring incidents and system performance.

    • Ensure appropriate backup and recovery arrangements.

    • Review system security and access controls.

    3.2 IT Executive / IT Support Engineer

    • Monitor POS system availability.

    • Provide first-level technical support.

    • Troubleshoot POS terminals, printers, network connectivity, and application issues.

    • Coordinate with the POS vendor for unresolved problems.

    • Maintain technical documentation and incident records.

    • Verify system functionality after maintenance or changes.

    • Assist authorized departments with approved configuration activities.

    3.3 Food & Beverage Manager

    • Ensure operational compliance with POS procedures.

    • Approve or recommend POS user access according to departmental authority.

    • Ensure staff receive appropriate system training.

    • Report operational issues to IT.

    • Validate outlet-level functionality and billing workflows.

    3.4 Restaurant / Outlet Manager

    • Monitor POS operations within the assigned outlet.

    • Ensure correct order entry and billing practices.

    • Report POS issues promptly.

    • Verify printer, order routing, and billing functionality.

    • Ensure staff use individual authorized accounts.

    3.5 Finance / Accounts Department

    • Monitor POS transaction and revenue reconciliation.

    • Verify billing and payment-related discrepancies.

    • Coordinate with IT regarding financial reporting or transaction integration issues.

    • Approve financial configuration changes where required.

    3.6 POS System Vendor

    • Provide application-level technical support.

    • Assist with software errors and advanced troubleshooting.

    • Support approved upgrades and configuration changes.

    • Assist with database and application recovery.

    • Provide technical documentation and escalation support.

    4. POS SYSTEM ACCESS MANAGEMENT

    4.1 New User Creation

    1. Receive a formal POS access request from the authorized department head or manager.

    2. Verify the employee's identity and department.

    3. Confirm the required outlet and job role.

    4. Obtain approval from the designated authority.

    5. Create the user account using the approved POS administration process.

    6. Assign only the permissions necessary for the employee's job responsibilities.

    7. Configure an individual user ID and secure authentication method.

    8. Avoid sharing administrator or employee credentials.

    9. Ask the department to verify the account's functionality.

    10. Record the account creation in the POS access register.

    4.2 User Role and Permission Modification

    1. Receive an approved request for the required modification.

    2. Verify the employee's current role and revised responsibilities.

    3. Review the requested permissions.

    4. Obtain approval from the authorized department head.

    5. Modify the required permissions.

    6. Verify that unrelated permissions have not been granted.

    7. Test the updated access with the relevant department.

    8. Update the access register.

    4.3 User Deactivation

    1. Receive an employee separation, transfer, or access-removal notification.

    2. Verify the employee details and effective date.

    3. Obtain confirmation from HR or the authorized department.

    4. Disable or deactivate the POS account within the required timeframe.

    5. Revoke any associated privileged access.

    6. Preserve transaction audit records according to hotel retention policy.

    7. Update the POS access register.

    Important: Individual POS accounts must not be shared between employees. Privileged accounts must be restricted to authorized personnel.

    5. DAILY POS SYSTEM MONITORING

    The IT team should perform daily checks to confirm that POS services are operational.

    5.1 Application Availability

    1. Verify that the POS application is accessible.

    2. Confirm that the application server or cloud service is available.

    3. Check for error messages or unexpected application behaviour.

    4. Confirm that authorized users can log in.

    5. Review available system alerts and logs.

    5.2 POS Terminal Verification

    1. Check the operational status of active POS terminals.

    2. Confirm that each terminal can connect to the POS application.

    3. Verify that the correct outlet and terminal configuration is displayed.

    4. Check date and time synchronization.

    5. Confirm that no unauthorized software or configuration changes are present.

    5.3 Printer Verification

    1. Check receipt printers and kitchen printers.

    2. Confirm paper availability.

    3. Verify printer connectivity.

    4. Print a test receipt where operationally appropriate.

    5. Confirm that kitchen orders are reaching the correct printer or display system.

    6. Report recurring printer issues to the IT team.

    5.4 PMS Integration Verification

    Where POS is integrated with the hotel PMS:

    1. Confirm that the POS can communicate with the PMS.

    2. Verify that authorized room-charge transactions can be processed.

    3. Confirm that room and guest information is being retrieved correctly.

    4. Review any failed or pending transaction messages.

    5. Coordinate with Front Office and Finance if a transaction discrepancy is identified.

    5.5 Network Connectivity

    1. Verify network connectivity at POS locations.

    2. Check wired or wireless connections, depending on the installation.

    3. Confirm that required application services are reachable.

    4. Report network interruptions to the network support team.

    5. Avoid making unauthorized network configuration changes.

    6. POS ORDER AND BILLING SUPPORT

    The IT team is responsible for technical support of POS order and billing functionality. Operational decisions regarding order entry, discounts, voids, and financial adjustments remain with authorized Food & Beverage and Finance personnel.

    6.1 Order Entry Issues

    If a user reports that orders cannot be entered:

    1. Confirm whether the issue affects one terminal or multiple terminals.

    2. Verify POS application availability.

    3. Check terminal connectivity.

    4. Confirm that the user account is active.

    5. Review relevant application error messages.

    6. Restart the application only when appropriate and after considering active transactions.

    7. Escalate unresolved application errors to the POS vendor.

    8. Ask the outlet to verify order entry after resolution.

    6.2 Billing Issues

    If a billing issue is reported:

    1. Obtain the transaction reference, outlet, terminal, and approximate transaction time.

    2. Identify the reported error.

    3. Check application connectivity and relevant transaction status.

    4. Review available logs or transaction records.

    5. Coordinate with Finance and the outlet manager to verify the transaction.

    6. Avoid editing, deleting, or recreating financial transactions without proper authorization.

    7. Escalate application-level billing errors to the vendor.

    8. Document the resolution.

    6.3 Discount, Void, and Refund Issues

    1. Verify whether the issue is related to system permissions or application functionality.

    2. Confirm that the employee has the appropriate authorization.

    3. Refer financial approval requirements to the designated departmental authority.

    4. Do not independently approve or process financial adjustments as an IT activity.

    5. Record technical changes and support actions.

    6. Escalate suspicious or unauthorized activity through the hotel's established control process.

    7. POS PRINTER AND KITCHEN DISPLAY SYSTEM SUPPORT

    7.1 Receipt Printer Troubleshooting

    1. Confirm that the printer is powered on.

    2. Check paper roll availability and correct installation.

    3. Check printer cable or network connectivity.

    4. Verify that the correct printer is selected in the POS configuration.

    5. Print a test receipt.

    6. Check for paper jams or hardware warnings.

    7. Restart the printer if safe and appropriate.

    8. Replace faulty consumables or arrange hardware repair.

    9. Record repeated failures.

    7.2 Kitchen Printer Troubleshooting

    1. Confirm that the kitchen printer is powered on.

    2. Verify network or cable connectivity.

    3. Check the printer queue, where available.

    4. Confirm that the relevant outlet and order category are correctly mapped.

    5. Verify whether orders are reaching other kitchen printers.

    6. Test order routing with the authorized outlet representative.

    7. Escalate application configuration issues to the POS vendor.

    7.3 Kitchen Display System (KDS)

    1. Confirm that the display is powered on.

    2. Verify network connectivity.

    3. Check application login and display status.

    4. Confirm that new orders are appearing.

    5. Verify order routing and status updates.

    6. Escalate application or integration problems to the vendor.

    8. POS AND PMS INTEGRATION MANAGEMENT

    Where POS and PMS are integrated, IT must ensure that the communication between the systems remains operational.

    8.1 Integration Monitoring

    1. Monitor the availability of the POS-PMS interface.

    2. Check for failed, delayed, or pending transactions.

    3. Verify that room-charge posting functionality is available.

    4. Review integration error logs where accessible.

    5. Coordinate with Front Office and Finance for transaction verification.

    8.2 Room Charge Posting Issues

    If room charges cannot be posted:

    1. Confirm that the guest room information is available in the POS.

    2. Verify whether the issue affects one guest or multiple guests.

    3. Check POS and PMS connectivity.

    4. Confirm the status of the integration service.

    5. Coordinate with Front Office to verify the guest account and posting eligibility.

    6. Do not bypass financial or guest verification controls.

    7. Escalate unresolved interface problems to the vendor.

    8. Verify the transaction status after recovery.

    8.3 Integration Failure

    If the interface stops working:

    1. Identify the time when the failure started.

    2. Determine which outlets and transactions are affected.

    3. Notify the IT Manager, Front Office, F&B, and Finance as appropriate.

    4. Contact the POS or PMS vendor according to the support arrangement.

    5. Follow the approved downtime procedure.

    6. Ensure that pending transactions are reconciled after service restoration.

    9. POS DOWNTIME MANAGEMENT

    POS downtime may occur due to application failure, network interruption, server problems, power failure, database errors, or cloud service outages.

    9.1 Initial Response

    1. Receive the downtime complaint.

    2. Record the time and affected outlet or terminals.

    3. Determine whether the issue is local or system-wide.

    4. Check power, network connectivity, application availability, and relevant services.

    5. Inform the IT Manager and affected department heads.

    6. Contact the vendor if the issue is application-related.

    9.2 Business Continuity

    1. Inform the Food & Beverage Manager and affected outlet managers.

    2. Activate the hotel's approved manual billing or contingency process.

    3. Use only authorized manual transaction records.

    4. Maintain clear transaction references and supporting details.

    5. Ensure that financial approvals remain in place.

    6. Avoid duplicate billing when the POS service becomes available again.

    9.3 Restoration

    1. Confirm that the underlying technical issue has been resolved.

    2. Verify application and terminal availability.

    3. Test order entry and billing.

    4. Verify printer and kitchen order routing.

    5. Test PMS integration where applicable.

    6. Reconcile manual transactions with system records.

    7. Obtain operational confirmation from the outlet manager.

    8. Close the downtime incident after documentation.

    10. POS SOFTWARE UPDATE AND CHANGE MANAGEMENT

    All POS application updates and configuration changes must follow the hotel's approved change management process.

    10.1 Software Updates

    1. Receive the update notification or vendor recommendation.

    2. Review the purpose, impact, and compatibility of the update.

    3. Coordinate with the vendor and relevant departments.

    4. Obtain approval from the designated authority.

    5. Confirm that the required backup or recovery arrangement is available.

    6. Schedule the update during an approved maintenance window.

    7. Notify affected departments in advance.

    8. Apply the update through the authorized procedure.

    9. Test application login, order entry, billing, printing, and integrations.

    10. Record the update details and test results.

    10.2 Configuration Changes

    Configuration changes may include:

    • Outlet creation or modification.

    • Printer mapping.

    • Menu category configuration.

    • Tax and service charge configuration.

    • User role changes.

    • Payment configuration.

    • POS-PMS interface settings.

    • Terminal configuration.

    Control: Menu prices, tax rules, service charges, payment settings, and other financial configurations must be approved by the authorized business or Finance authority. IT should implement only approved changes.

    11. POS BACKUP AND RECOVERY COORDINATION

    The IT team must ensure that appropriate POS data protection and recovery arrangements are maintained.

    11.1 Backup Coordination

    1. Identify whether the POS system is cloud-hosted or locally hosted.

    2. Confirm backup responsibilities with the vendor or hosting provider.

    3. Verify that the backup schedule meets hotel requirements.

    4. Review available backup success or failure reports.

    5. Escalate failed or missing backups.

    6. Maintain records of backup status and related incidents.

    11.2 Recovery Support

    1. Obtain authorization for the recovery activity.

    2. Contact the vendor or authorized system administrator.

    3. Confirm the appropriate recovery point.

    4. Assess the operational and financial impact of restoration.

    5. Perform recovery through the approved technical process.

    6. Validate application availability and transaction integrity.

    7. Coordinate with Finance and F&B for transaction reconciliation.

    8. Document the recovery activity.

    12. POS SECURITY MANAGEMENT

    The POS system may contain commercially sensitive information, transaction records, and guest-related information. Appropriate security controls must be maintained.

    12.1 User Security

    • Provide individual user accounts.

    • Apply role-based permissions.

    • Restrict administrator access.

    • Disable accounts promptly when access is no longer required.

    • Review privileged access periodically.

    • Do not share passwords or authentication credentials.

    12.2 Device Security

    • Restrict physical access to POS terminals.

    • Do not allow unauthorized software installation.

    • Apply approved operating system and security updates.

    • Protect POS devices from unauthorized USB or external device usage.

    • Secure unattended terminals.

    • Report lost or stolen devices immediately.

    12.3 Network Security

    • Place POS devices on an appropriately controlled network.

    • Restrict unnecessary network access.

    • Coordinate firewall and network rule changes through approved processes.

    • Avoid connecting unauthorized devices to POS network segments.

    • Monitor and report suspicious connectivity or access activity.

    12.4 Payment Security

    Where card payment terminals or payment applications are used:

    • Follow the hotel's payment security policy.

    • Use approved payment service providers and configurations.

    • Do not store card security codes or sensitive authentication data.

    • Restrict access to payment-related configuration.

    • Refer suspected payment security incidents to the designated security and Finance teams.

    • Follow applicable payment security requirements, including PCI DSS where relevant.

    13. VENDOR COORDINATION AND SUPPORT

    The IT Department must maintain an effective support relationship with the POS system vendor.

    13.1 Vendor Support Process

    1. Identify the issue and affected services.

    2. Perform initial troubleshooting.

    3. Collect relevant error messages, screenshots, logs, and timestamps, ensuring sensitive information is protected.

    4. Create a support ticket with the vendor.

    5. Record the ticket number and assigned support contact.

    6. Monitor progress until resolution.

    7. Test the system after vendor intervention.

    8. Obtain confirmation from the affected department.

    9. Close the incident with proper documentation.

    13.2 Vendor Escalation

    Escalate issues when:

    • POS services remain unavailable.

    • Financial transactions are affected.

    • PMS integration is interrupted.

    • Repeated application errors occur.

    • Data integrity is suspected.

    • A security incident is identified.

    • Vendor response exceeds the agreed service level.

    14. POS INCIDENT DOCUMENTATION

    The IT Department must maintain records of POS-related technical activities.

    Required records may include:

    1. POS user access register.

    2. POS terminal and device inventory.

    3. Daily POS monitoring checklist.

    4. POS incident and complaint register.

    5. Vendor support ticket register.

    6. Software update and change records.

    7. Backup verification records.

    8. POS-PMS integration incident records.

    9. Downtime and recovery reports.

    10. Preventive maintenance records.

    11. Security incident reports.

    12. Departmental training and handover records, where applicable.

    Records must be stored securely and retained according to hotel policy and applicable legal requirements.

    15. PREVENTIVE MAINTENANCE SCHEDULE

    Frequency Maintenance Activity Responsible Team
    Daily Check POS application availability IT
    Daily Verify active POS terminals IT / F&B
    Daily Check receipt and kitchen printers IT / Outlet
    Daily Review critical system alerts IT
    Daily Check POS-PMS integration status IT
    Weekly Inspect POS terminals and connected devices IT
    Weekly Review recurring technical incidents IT
    Weekly Check printer condition and connectivity IT
    Monthly Review POS user accounts and permissions IT / Department Head
    Monthly Review vendor tickets and recurring problems IT
    Monthly Verify backup status and reports IT / Vendor
    Quarterly Review system access and security controls IT
    Quarterly Review POS inventory and configuration records IT
    Annually Review this SOP and support arrangements IT Manager

    The schedule may be adjusted according to system architecture, operational requirements, vendor recommendations, and hotel policy.

    16. POS INCIDENT ESCALATION MATRIX

    Priority Example Issue Initial Response Escalation
    Critical All POS outlets unavailable Immediate IT Manager, F&B Head, Vendor
    High POS-PMS integration failure affecting room charges Immediate IT Manager, Front Office, Finance, Vendor
    High Multiple outlets unable to process orders or bills Immediate IT Manager, F&B Head, Vendor
    Medium Single POS terminal failure As soon as possible IT Executive / IT Manager
    Medium Kitchen printer or KDS failure As soon as possible IT / Outlet Manager
    Low Minor display or reporting issue Within normal support workflow IT Support
    Low User access or routine configuration request As per approval workflow IT / Department Head

    Actual response and resolution targets must follow the hotel's approved IT service levels and vendor agreements.

    17. POS PERFORMANCE MONITORING

    The IT Manager should periodically review the following indicators:

    • POS application availability.

    • Number of POS-related incidents.

    • Average incident resolution time.

    • Number of recurring terminal failures.

    • Printer and KDS failure frequency.

    • POS-PMS integration failure frequency.

    • Backup success rate, where applicable.

    • Number of unauthorized or inappropriate access incidents.

    • Vendor response and resolution performance.

    • Number of unresolved critical issues.

    Performance reports should be reviewed with relevant departments to identify recurring problems and improvement opportunities.

    18. DAILY POS OPERATIONS CHECKLIST

    IT Department Daily Checklist

    • Confirm POS application availability.

    • Verify active POS terminals.

    • Check terminal network connectivity.

    • Verify receipt printer functionality.

    • Verify kitchen printer and KDS functionality.

    • Check POS-PMS integration status, where applicable.

    • Review critical error messages and alerts.

    • Check reported POS complaints.

    • Review unresolved vendor tickets.

    • Update the POS incident register.

    • Confirm that critical issues have been escalated.

    Food & Beverage Department Checklist

    • Confirm that authorized staff can log in.

    • Verify order entry functionality.

    • Confirm that orders reach the correct kitchen printer or display.

    • Verify billing functionality.

    • Report any transaction or integration issue to IT.

    • Follow the approved downtime procedure when required.

    19. HOTEL-SPECIFIC IMPLEMENTATION NOTES

    This SOP is designed as a general framework and should be adapted to the hotel's POS environment.

    Examples of POS platforms used in the hospitality industry include:

    • Oracle MICROS Simphony.

    • Infor POS solutions.

    • IDS Next POS.

    • eZee POS.

    • Other cloud-based and locally hosted hospitality POS applications.

    The exact procedures may vary depending on the system and its configuration.

    Before implementation, the hotel should document:

    1. POS application name and version.

    2. Vendor and support contact details.

    3. Number and location of POS terminals.

    4. Restaurant, bar, banquet, and other outlet configurations.

    5. POS server or cloud hosting details.

    6. POS-PMS integration architecture.

    7. Payment terminal integration details.

    8. Backup and recovery responsibilities.

    9. Approved downtime and manual billing procedures.

    10. Departmental approval responsibilities.

    Important: System-specific instructions should be maintained as implementation notes or supporting documents. The core SOP should remain consistent across the hotel's POS operations.

    20. COMPLIANCE AND EXCEPTIONS

    All departments using the POS system must follow the approved procedures described in this SOP.

    Any exception must be:

    • Documented with a clear reason.

    • Reviewed by the relevant department head.

    • Approved by the authorized management representative.

    • Recorded by IT where technical access or configuration is affected.

    Any suspected unauthorized access, transaction manipulation, data loss, or payment security incident must be reported through the hotel's established incident reporting process.

    21. SOP REVIEW AND REVISION HISTORY

    This SOP must be reviewed at least annually or whenever a major change occurs in the POS application, infrastructure, integration, security requirements, or hotel operating procedures.
